About

October 10, 2026 · Songrui LI
I am an MSCA Doctoral Candidate in the Parallel CFD & Optimization Unit at the National Technical University of Athens. Within the FairCFD Doctoral Network, I develop shadowing-based sensitivity and adjoint methods for chaotic flows, with applications to automotive aerodynamic optimization in collaboration with Toyota Gazoo Racing.

After completing my MSc at Imperial College London, I spent three years at SAIC Volkswagen working on automotive CFD and scientific machine learning. I then joined Vanderbilt University to study fluid–structure interaction in biomedical applications. I now work with the team behind OpenFOAM’s adjointShapeOptimizationFoam, extending my focus to sensitivity analysis and optimization for complex engineering flows.

Alongside this work, I explore how Scientific Machine Learning and AI-assisted workflows can improve our understanding of nonlinear flow dynamics and support efficient, physically consistent simulations.
