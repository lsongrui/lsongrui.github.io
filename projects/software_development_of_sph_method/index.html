Overview#

I extended DualSPHysics, an open-source smoothed particle hydrodynamics (SPH) solver, in C++/CUDA. The project introduced surface-tension and aerodynamic-drag effects for GPU-accelerated automotive rainwater simulations.

The work formed part of a collaboration between SAIC Volkswagen and Northwestern Polytechnical University. I led a team of four, working on model implementation and code structure, with academic supervision from Prof. Zhe Ji.

Engineering problem#

Rainwater can travel along a vehicle surface and enter areas where it causes an unwanted experience. One example is opening a wet trunk after rainfall. Predicting this motion helps engineers assess water-management behavior during design.

Water trajectories over the vehicle body.

Aerodynamic drag#

The air-drag extension enabled one-way air–water coupling. The implementation calculates particle drag using the exposed area and the local flow information. The algorithm includes a treatment of occluded area when estimating the area exposed to the surrounding air.
